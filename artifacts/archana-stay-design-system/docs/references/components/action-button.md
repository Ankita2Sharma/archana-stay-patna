## Extracted behavior and styling

Buttons are pill-shaped, compact, and action-oriented. The primary variant is
deep navy with light text and a soft lift shadow. The gold variant is reserved
for high-intent or highlighted actions. Ghost buttons use a light border and
transparent surface. Small buttons reduce both padding and type size.

Hover states lift the control slightly; they do not introduce a new visual
language.
