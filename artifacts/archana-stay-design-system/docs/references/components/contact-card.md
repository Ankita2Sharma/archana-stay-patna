## Extracted behavior and styling

Contact details use a bordered card with a Fraunces heading, softly separated
person rows, circular initials, and compact circular actions. The visual system
keeps the owner visible and approachable. A full-width enquiry action follows
the people rows.

The phone numbers, names, and enquiry destination are product data and must
remain in the consuming app.
